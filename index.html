About Me

A psychologist with a Bachelor's degree in General Psychology and professional experience across clinical, humanitarian, and educational settings.

I have experience in Mental Health and Psychosocial Support (MHPSS), psychological counseling, psychosocial assessment, and supporting children, adolescents, refugees, and other vulnerable populations. My work includes individual and group counseling, identifying psychosocial needs, developing individualized support plans, and facilitating mental health awareness and psychoeducational activities.

My professional approach incorporates CBT-informed and DBT-informed techniques, Trauma-Informed Care, and Psychological First Aid (PFA), within my professional competence. I am committed to ethical practice, confidentiality, safeguarding, cultural sensitivity, and person-centered care. I aim to contribute to accessible mental health services, psychological resilience, and effective humanitarian and community-based programs.
